# science-facts-hub-phase-4-
A daily journey exploring fascinating science facts around the universe. Phase 4 (Days 51-75).
## 🔬 Science Facts Hub — Phase 4 (Day 51) 🚀
Did you know **honey never spoils**? 🍯♾️
- **Eternal Shelf-Life:** Archaeologists found 3,000-year-old honey in Egyptian tombs that is still edible!
- **Natural Preservative:** Low moisture and high acidity stop bacteria from growing.
## 🔬 Science Facts Hub — Phase 4 (Day 52) 🚀
Did you know **human bones are stronger than steel**? 🦴⚡
- **Strength Ratio:** Ounce for ounce, bone is stronger than steel.
- **High Resistance:** A cubic inch of bone can bear a load of 19,000 lbs (~8,600 kg).
- **Flexible Structure:** Its unique mineral structure absorbs shock without breaking easily.
## 🔬 Science Facts Hub — Phase 4 (Day 53) 🚀
Did you know **a cloud can weigh over a million pounds**? ☁️⚖️
- **Massive Weight:** An average cumulus cloud weighs about 1.1 million lbs (~500,000 kg).
- **Floating Secret:** It stays afloat because the warm air below it is denser than the cloud itself.
- **Micro-Drops:** The weight is spread out over trillions of microscopic water droplets.
